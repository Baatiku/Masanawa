Risk & Compliance

Risk and Compliance Associate

Run day-to-day KYC and AML operations, keep our policies working in practice and coordinate with legal counsel on regulatory matters.

Work arrangement: Remote · NigeriaEmployment type: ConsultingLevel: Senior
Apply for this role

About the role

A payments company is trusted only if it knows its customers and acts on risk. This role runs that work every day: identity verification, reviews of unusual activity, policy and the records that show we did it properly. You will work with legal counsel on regulatory questions rather than replace them.

What you will own

  • KYC and AML operations, including reviews of flagged accounts.
  • Compliance policies and how they are applied in our tools and processes.
  • Provider due diligence and compliance reporting.

Responsibilities

  • Review flagged accounts and transactions and record decisions with reasons.
  • Keep policies current and make sure the product follows them.
  • Prepare documentation for partners, providers and regulators with counsel.
  • Advise the team on the risk in new products before they launch.

What success looks like

  • Within your first quarter, flagged-account reviews run on a documented process with clear turnaround.
  • Policies match what the product actually does, and the evidence is easy to produce.

What you need

  • Experience in KYC, AML or compliance at a fintech, bank or payments company.
  • Sound judgement and careful record keeping.
  • Clear writing for policies and case notes.

Nice to have

  • A recognised compliance certification.
  • Familiarity with Nigerian regulation of payments and digital assets.
  • Experience with the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

Working arrangement

  • Remote, Nigeria
  • Consulting
