Questions before you start.

Straight answers on prices, speed and safety.

  • A Nigerian app for crypto and everyday payments, all in naira. You can buy crypto and gas for your own wallet on 30+ networks, sell crypto for naira, withdraw to your bank, pay for airtime, data, electricity, cable TV and exam PINs, and send Telegram Stars, Premium and Gifts.

  • Every account gets its own account number. Transfer naira to it from any Nigerian bank and your balance updates when the transfer clears. You can also deposit supported crypto, which is credited in naira at the live rate.

  • Gas, Telegram Stars and Premium usually arrive within a minute. Bills are usually instant. Withdrawals to your bank usually land within minutes. Crypto deposits are credited once the network confirms them.

  • Every on-chain delivery is recorded with its transaction hash and the address it went to, so you can check it yourself on a block explorer.

  • You see the full price before you confirm. For crypto, the rate and any network fee are shown on the review screen. A withdrawal shows its fee and the amount that will land. There is no charge to open an account.

  • You can sign up with your email and phone number. Identity verification is requested for higher limits and before some services, such as withdrawing to a bank account.

  • If a payment fails, the money goes back to your balance. If the result is unclear, for example the provider has not replied yet, we hold the money and check first. Support can always see where it is.

  • Make a purchase every day: gas, a bill, Telegram Stars or Premium, or a crypto deposit. On day 3, 7, 14 and 30 you unlock a cash reward you can claim into your wallet. Miss a day and you get one grace day to catch up, once per cycle. Pay Streak is on the web app.

  • Share your link or code. When a friend signs up and makes a first qualifying purchase, you get a cash reward, then 0.2% of their qualifying purchases after that. Earnings can be redeemed to your balance once they reach ₦500.

  • Email support@nasfampay.com, message us on WhatsApp at +234 806 047 0874, or use the help centre. Include your transaction reference and we can find it quickly.
