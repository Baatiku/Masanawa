Our story

One account for the money you move every day

Buying crypto with naira, paying the network fee a transfer needs, topping up airtime and sending Telegram Stars often means a different app, a different balance and a different person to ask for each.

That is hard to follow, and a mistake can cost money. Nasfam Pay began with gas: the network fee every crypto transfer needs, paid in naira. The same account now buys and sells crypto, withdraws to Nigerian banks, pays bills and sends Telegram Stars, Premium and Gifts, with one balance, one history and one place to get help.

  1. 1

    Reserved

    Your naira is set aside first.

    The moment you approve, the amount leaves your spendable balance in one database step, before anything is sent. The same naira cannot pay for two things.

    On your receipt: Amount paid, Paid from

  2. 2

    Sent

    One payment at a time.

    Your account runs one payment at a time, and a new one cannot start while the last is still pending. Each order is marked as sent before it goes to the network.

    On your receipt: Recipient, Amount sent

  3. 3

    Confirmed

    Delivery is checked on the network.

    A transfer that has been sent has not always arrived. We keep checking the network until it confirms, and your receipt shows the transaction hash with a link to the block explorer.

    On your receipt: Transaction hash, View on explorer

  4. 4

    Settled

    Unclear means held, not guessed.

    A confirmed transfer is done. A failed one goes back to your balance. If the result is unclear, the naira stays held and is checked again, day and night, before any of it moves.

    On your receipt: Status

What we are, and what we are not

The same position as our terms of service, in fewer words.

We are
We are not

We are

Registered in Nigeria

Nasfam Enterprises, CAC registration 7791264, operates Nasfam Pay.

We are not

A bank

Your balance is not a bank deposit and is not insured under any deposit protection scheme.

We are

Holding your balance in naira

Your balance is a record of the naira we owe you.

We are not

Holding your crypto after delivery

Once it reaches the wallet address you gave, only you control it. We cannot recover or reverse it.

We are

The other side of your trade

We sell you the crypto, and we buy it from you.

We are not

A broker

We do not act on your behalf in anyone else’s market.

We are

Watching for fraud

If our checks flag a serious risk, money going out can pause while we look. Money coming in is generally not blocked, so funds are kept safe while we check.

We are not

An adviser

Nothing we publish tells you to buy, sell or hold. Crypto prices can fall sharply.

We are

Careful with your data

Handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

We are not

Selling your data

We do not sell personal data.

How we work

What we hold ourselves to

Four rules, each one something you can see the product do.

  1. 01

    Build for reliability

    A payment is set aside before it is sent. If a network has not answered, the money is held and checked, never guessed.

  2. 02

    Keep finance understandable

    You see the full price before you pay, and every receipt says what happened in plain words.

  3. 03

    Move quickly, verify carefully

    A crypto delivery is marked complete only once the network confirms it, with the hash on your receipt to check.

  4. 04

    Serve real customer needs

    Naira in and out, bills and Telegram from one balance, and a person on WhatsApp when something needs checking.
