2.1 The nature of the service

Nasfam Pay is a technology platform that lets you exchange Naira for Digital Assets and Digital Assets for Naira, and pay for a defined set of digital goods and utility services. When you buy a Digital Asset, we deliver it to a Wallet Address that you nominate and control. When you sell, we pay Naira to a Nigerian bank account in your name.

We are a counterparty to the exchange you request. We are not a broker acting on your behalf, an investment adviser, a portfolio manager, a custodian of your Digital Assets after delivery, or a bank.

We do not hold Digital Assets for you after delivery. Once an asset reaches the Wallet Address you provided, it is under your exclusive control and we can neither recover it, reverse it, nor access it. Custody, security, and backup of your wallet and its keys are entirely your responsibility.

Your Naira Wallet balance is a record of value we owe you in Naira. It is not a deposit with a bank and it is not insured under any deposit protection scheme.

Nothing on the Platform is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and nothing should be read as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset. Prices, rate displays, market data, and educational material are provided for information only. Decisions you make are yours alone.

6. Buying, Selling and Swapping

6.1 Quotes and rates

Rates are quoted live and are valid only for the short period shown at the point of quotation. The rate you see when you confirm is the rate applied to that transaction. Rates include our margin, and the fee applicable to a transaction is displayed before you approve it.

Digital asset prices move quickly. A quote that expires before you confirm must be requested again, and the new rate may be better or worse.

6.2 The address you provide is final

You are solely responsible for the Wallet Address and Network you enter. The Platform validates address format and screens certain destinations, but format validation cannot tell us whether an address belongs to you.

Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed once broadcast and confirmed. If you provide an address you do not control, an address on the wrong Network, an exchange address that does not support the asset, or a smart contract that cannot release funds, the assets will be lost and neither we nor anyone else can recover them. Check the address and the Network before you confirm.

6.3 Delivery, delay and failure

We broadcast transactions promptly after payment is confirmed. Confirmation time then depends on the Network, which we do not control. Congestion, outages, forks, reorganisations, validator behaviour, and node provider failures can delay or prevent settlement.

Where a transaction cannot be completed, we return the amount to your Naira Wallet. Where a transaction is broadcast but its outcome is unclear, we hold the position and reconcile it against the chain before crediting or refunding, so that value is neither paid twice nor lost. This may take longer than a normal transaction, and we will not guess at an outcome in order to settle faster.

6.4 Sales to your bank account

When you sell a Digital Asset, we pay Naira to a Nigerian bank account that has been verified against the name on your Account. We may decline to pay to an account in a different name.

Settlement depends on the banking system and the payout partner. Where a payout fails or is returned, we credit the amount back to your Naira Wallet.

6.5 Swaps and third-party execution

Swap services are executed through an external exchange partner. Rates are estimates until the partner confirms, and the amount finally received may differ from the estimate. Minimum and maximum amounts, supported pairs, and processing times are set by that partner. Where a swap fails or falls outside the partner's accepted range, the partner's refund process applies and we will assist you in pursuing it.

6.6 Errors and mispricing

If a rate, price, or balance is displayed incorrectly because of a technical fault, a data feed error, or human error, and a reasonable person would recognise the figure as obviously wrong, we may cancel or reverse the affected transaction and restore the position that would have existed had the error not occurred. We will tell you when we do this.
