What SOL pays for on Solana.

Every Solana transaction pays its fee in SOL. A wallet with none cannot sign anything, whatever else it holds.

  • Swaps on Jupiter and Raydium

    Every buy, sell and swap pays its network fee in SOL.

  • Selling a memecoin

    Holding the token is not enough. Selling it on Pump.fun or a DEX still needs SOL for the fee.

  • Sending USDC or USDT

    SPL tokens move on Solana, so the fee is paid in SOL, never in the token.

  • Claims and mints

    An airdrop claim or an NFT mint is a transaction too, and each one needs SOL.

How to buy SOL gas in Nigeria

  • The small amount of SOL every Solana transaction pays the network. It comes out of the SOL in your wallet whenever you swap, send or mint.

  • Selling is a Solana transaction, and every Solana transaction pays its fee in SOL. The token you want to sell cannot pay it.

  • Any Solana wallet you control, such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Paste its address at checkout and the app checks it is a Solana wallet address before you can pay.

  • The least is 0.008 SOL or ₦1,000, whichever costs more, and the most is ₦2,000,000 per purchase. The price section on this page shows today’s naira minimum.

  • From your naira balance. You add naira by bank transfer from any Nigerian bank to your own account number, with no dollar card.

  • Your receipt carries the transaction hash and a link to it on Solscan, so you can check it yourself.

  • If the network rejects it, the naira goes back to your balance. If the result is unclear, the money is held until it has been checked. You can report any transaction from its receipt.
