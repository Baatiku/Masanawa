# Masanawa contributor and coding-agent rules (updated 2026-10-20)

**Read the [one active product roadmap](docs/PRODUCT-ROADMAP-20261019.md), [screen/journey contract](docs/SCREEN-JOURNEYS.md) and [current handoff](CONTINUE-HERE.md) before working.** Older dated product plans, historic handoffs and Alpha/Twin tools have been removed from the current tree. Do not recreate an archive or restore their superseded UX rules.

1. Product (owner revision 2026-10-20): human and Masana communication/creators; **Chats · Updates · Wallet · Calls · Rooms**, Chats default. **Updates is the Facebook-style mixed human/Masana scrolling post feed**, NOT WhatsApp ephemeral Status/rings; preserve the existing video player and authorized video source but remove a primary Videos tab from the target Updates UI. Legacy `status` routes/deep links remain compatibility aliases. Use one original accessible Masanawa green/white theme; secondary details do not become extra root tabs.
1a. Keep one creator-configurable Rooms product rather than separate Group/Channel/Community products. Ordinary human Updates publishing is a **paid MSNW action** only after a real, approved server fee quote, explicit review and exactly-once ledger-authorized charge/reconciliation; the fee amount is currently NOT DECIDED. Reading/liking/commenting is not charged. P2P is important individual-user trading with one canonical human public/trader profile and real verified escrow; **NO cards, merchant onboarding, merchant storefront, merchant POS/checkout or merchant commerce**. Follow [complete screen blueprint](docs/COMPLETE-SCREEN-AND-JOURNEY-BLUEPRINT.md), [Updates](docs/UPDATES-FEED-FINAL-SPEC.md), [P2P/trader profile](docs/P2P-AND-PROFILE-FINAL-SPEC.md) and [Rooms](docs/UNIFIED-ROOMS-PRODUCT-SPEC.md) before touching screens. Competitor candidates are for audit/disposition, not clutter or automatic implementation.
2. Preserve users and finances: **MSNW** only as customer-facing Wallet/token identity. Historical Credits/Cr/MSN liabilities remain intact, separately reconciled; never rename into MSNW, wipe/transfer or claim supply based on UI changes. On-chain token source is not proof of mainnet deployment.
3. Creator ownership: one successfully published Masana free for account lifetime; next publication default 5,000 MSNW, server-quoted and captured once under finance controls. Creation/voice/earnings/permission authority remains server-side. Bodi male and Fora female approved voices, no user-visible blanket promise of voice cloning.
4. Security: canonical ledger/task/auth/media contracts and exact account/session fencing. Every money, asset, provider or escrow operation needs server-bound authorization, idempotency, signed/verified external proof, timeouts, reconciliation, post-action readback and durable receipt. Models may propose; users authorize consequential actions. Payment screenshots/marked-paid flags alone cannot release escrow.
5. Financial geography: international users, but rails restricted by actual eligibility, jurisdiction, provider agreement and capability. No implicit fiat peg/direct fiat withdrawal; do not promise country support from currency formatting alone.
6. Safety: no secrets, PII or real keys in source, logs, tests or PRs. No unqualified production issuance, paid side effects, destructive migrations or irreversible deletion. Preserve encrypted backups, telemetry, account deletion and rollback obligations.
7. Before coding: inspect fresh main, open PRs/issues/claims and affected code/tests. Own exact paths; shared router/financial ledger/migration registry/deployment one integrator. Never overwrite another contributor's head, force push a shared branch, or delete unfinished source.
8. For UI work: specify page tree, nav/back/deep-link, controls and all loading/empty/error/offline/permission/account-switch states from SCREEN-JOURNEYS first. Prefer minimal working flows, reusable Flutter components, accessibility, small screens, RTL, low data.
9. For delivery: source + test in scoped PR; run reachable TS/Vitest/PostgreSQL/Flutter/Android checks, container fallback where runner unavailable, honest NOT_RUN labels. Merge compatible completed development promptly, with actual security/test failure repaired. Owner does final acceptance; do not equate integration with VERIFIED_E2E or DEPLOYED_VERIFIED.
10. Source-of-truth order: executable behavior at exact SHA → roadmap/owner decisions → current technical/API/security/financial contracts → actual PR evidence. No historical branch, Git commit or external document may override current owner authorization.
11. Keep approved implementation practical: no duplicate backend providers/ledgers, unnecessary microservices, expensive dormant services, forced custom voice onboarding, synthetic fund claims or undisclosed hidden fees.
12. Handoff must identify base/head SHA, actual PR, touched paths, phase/task IDs, relevant commands/results, current deployment status, remaining source/verification, next action and safe merge target. Update checkboxes **in the same roadmap**, not new dated checklists.

13. Owner revision (2026-10-20): human messaging/calls and ONE user-created, creator-configurable **Room** product are approved (group/community/channel/topic abilities are settings within a Room, not separate products). Contacts tabs **Masana / Humans**, one canonical human social/P2P profile; visible **Updates** is the persistent mixed feed with paid human posting, not stories. No extra tab or merchant/card flows. Follow revised Phases 4–8; preserve actual source/device/provider work carried from earlier phases.
