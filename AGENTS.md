1. **Only five root tabs:** Chats (one-to-one Masana), Status (platform-Masana authored posts with human likes/comments/follows/saves/shares/tips), Wallet (integer MB), Calls (one-to-one Masana audio), Data (Flutterwave whole-GB mobile data redemption).

3. **Remove from reachable UI/API/runtime:** human direct messages/calls, Rooms, videos, human publishing, public Masana creation/creator sales, multi-Masana/group calls, P2P escrow/trading, blockchain/crypto/MSNW customer flows, credit sales, swaps, withdrawals, non-data bills, retired workers/web flows.
4. **MB wallet plus separate NGN funding balance (owner update, 25 October):** New verified deposits settle in integer-kobo NGN, then an explicitly reviewed, atomic package exchange debits NGN and credits purchased MB. Keep cash and MB as distinct liabilities; no withdrawal/P2P cash flow. Never auto-convert a deposit or migrate historical money. **MB ledger:** 1 GB = 1000 MB; show three-decimal-trimmed GB and exact MB, track purchased, restricted welcome, reserved, immutable entries, idempotency and receipts. Never transform historic MSN, Credits or MSNW balances into MB. Preserve historic finance records and liabilities while disabling new legacy writes and planning forward-only retirement migrations.
5. **Welcome credit:** 500 MB once for eligible phone-verified identity, limited to successful Masana replies or connected call time; not Send, Tip or Redeem. Protect against reinstall/reverified-phone double grants. One successful Masana reply costs 1 MB, failed reply 0; one started connected call second costs 1 MB, ringing/setup 0. Reserve before provider work, reconcile provider-confirmed outcome and release unused reserved MB.
6. **Transfer, Request and tips:** whole-MB, verified target user/character, explicit review, atomic account movement, original idempotency key, bounded/single-payment request with seven-day expiry, durable receipt, transactionally enforced balance restriction. Do not create human chat. Masana tip balances cannot withdraw or redeem.
