## Product contract and pricing (supersedes only the earlier blanket human-publishing prohibition)
- Exactly five root tabs remain: **Home (renamed existing Status screen), Chats, Wallet, Calls, Data**. Same five tabs only: Home moves to the far-left position and opens by default; Chats moves second. Preserve `/status/:postId` links and `?tab=status` compatibility. No human chats/calls, free user feed posting, Rooms, creator marketplace, video composer or extra social tabs.
- Status mixes **approved platform-Masana posts** with **admin-approved, user-funded text/image promotional posts**. Humans cannot publish an unpaid post. Preserve actual human author identity and display the authentic human publisher without adding a default **Sponsored** badge or replacement promotional chip; never impersonate a Masana. Applicable legal disclosure must be evaluated before deployment, separately from the owner-rejected badge design.
- **Any signed-in user can tap Boost on any eligible approved public post**, including official Masana content and another user's post. A booster funds only their own separately receipted view budget; the original owner retains all content, moderation and deletion rights. Multiple boosts share one canonical post and view count, with no double billing. Each boost is **up to N additional qualified views at 1 MB/view** from eligible MB. Reuse existing Recharge/Add MB and wallet activity.
- **Budget exhaustion:** As soon as all eligible paid budgets for a **human paid promotional post** are exhausted, it leaves Home/search/recommendations immediately and enters a server-timed **72-hour disabled grace** visible in its owner's profile/management area, with Boost Again and Delete. Valid funded reboost within grace cancels expiry and restores an approved post; at 72 hours purge post content and controlled media/cache/index projections, leaving minimal immutable financial/audit receipts. **Official Masana editorial posts are not auto-deleted by a third party's boost ending**: their paid boost placement stops, but their independently approved editorial feed eligibility remains until admin retires it. Hidden/rejected/unsafe posts cannot be reactivated by spending MB.
- **Signup geography:** The email-OTP signup contact phone uses an editable country picker with prefilled international dial code and a national-number field; a parsed number suggests country, but the user confirms it. Load appropriate State/Province/Region choices from that country's subdivision list. **A phone number does not identify a person's state or necessarily their actual residence**; the user chooses the subdivision. Persist user-confirmed ISO country/subdivision codes separately from phone origin and never silently enable foreign data/funding providers or ad targeting.
- User purchases a **maximum qualified-view target** at **1 MB per qualified view**, reserved from eligible, actually funded MB before moderation. This is a prepaid **up-to** view target, not guaranteed reach or a time-bound delivery promise.
- Direct/organic qualified views allocate **1 MB to the platform**; unique qualified views attributed to another user's signed sharing link allocate **0.5 MB to the sharer and 0.5 MB to the platform**. Post owners do not earn commissions from their own campaign. No multilevel referral rewards.
- Support fractional accrual in integer **half-MB units**, while existing wallet transfers/redemptions remain whole MB; two valid referred views earn 1 MB and fractions carry forward without rounding away the liability.
- **No paid-post display pacing, cooldown, waiting period, frequency cap, artificial interleaving ratio, or early audience cap.** Approved, funded promotions are eligible for immediate ordinary feed display, subject only to normal feed pagination, content/account safety, total purchased view budget and actual available viewers. Do not introduce the earlier proposed “one promotion per four/five Masana posts” rule. Moderation and provider/ledger safety remain mandatory; do not bypass either to obtain immediate display.
- Count verified unique views on **every** feed card (including unfunded Masana posts); only funded eligible campaigns charge MB or earn referral commission. If count is not authoritative, show an honest unavailable/pending state, never a fabricated 0.
- All rewards/redemptions must be covered by exact server-owned ledgers and provider-approved data delivery. Restricted welcome grants cannot fund promotions or be redeemed. No cash withdrawals or speculative MB earnings.
