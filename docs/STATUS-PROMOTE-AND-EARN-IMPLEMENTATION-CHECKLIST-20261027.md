## Product contract and pricing (supersedes only the earlier blanket human-publishing prohibition)
- Exactly five root tabs remain: Chats, Status, Wallet, Calls, Data. No human chats/calls, free user feed posting, Rooms, creator marketplace, video composer or extra social tabs.
- Status mixes **approved platform-Masana posts** with **admin-approved, user-funded text/image promotional posts**. Humans cannot publish an unpaid post. Preserve actual human author identity and visibly label paid posts **Sponsored**; never impersonate a Masana.
- User purchases a **maximum qualified-view target** at **1 MB per qualified view**, reserved from eligible, actually funded MB before moderation. This is a prepaid **up-to** view target, not guaranteed reach or a time-bound delivery promise.
- Direct/organic qualified views allocate **1 MB to the platform**; unique qualified views attributed to another user's signed sharing link allocate **0.5 MB to the sharer and 0.5 MB to the platform**. Post owners do not earn commissions from their own campaign. No multilevel referral rewards.
- Support fractional accrual in integer **half-MB units**, while existing wallet transfers/redemptions remain whole MB; two valid referred views earn 1 MB and fractions carry forward without rounding away the liability.
- **No paid-post display pacing, cooldown, waiting period, frequency cap, artificial interleaving ratio, or early audience cap.** Approved, funded promotions are eligible for immediate ordinary feed display, subject only to normal feed pagination, content/account safety, total purchased view budget and actual available viewers. Do not introduce the earlier proposed “one promotion per four/five Masana posts” rule. Moderation and provider/ledger safety remain mandatory; do not bypass either to obtain immediate display.
- Count verified unique views on **every** feed card (including unfunded Masana posts); only funded eligible campaigns charge MB or earn referral commission. If count is not authoritative, show an honest unavailable/pending state, never a fabricated 0.
- All rewards/redemptions must be covered by exact server-owned ledgers and provider-approved data delivery. Restricted welcome grants cannot fund promotions or be redeemed. No cash withdrawals or speculative MB earnings.

Use one owner for shared feed widgets, one for money/migrations/financial composition and one for canonical journeys/router, coordinating merges; do not have independent branches repeatedly rewrite the same Status card. Each checked item requires code, focused tests, exact-head CI evidence and merged main (no owner-handset checkbox).

- [ ] **SPE-B04** Build one fast public text/image post landing page with actual approved content and app deep-link fallback; external guests need not install before viewing. Expired/removed/private content is denied, and no author/viewer PII appears in a referral URL.

- [ ] **SPE-B06** Commit per-view allocations atomically: 1 MB gross charge from campaign reserve; organic = 1 MB platform, referral = 0.5 MB pending sharer + 0.5 MB platform. Use integer half-MB internal units and idempotent event/ledger keys; prevent overdraw under simultaneous views.

- [ ] **SPE-B08** Separate pending, verified, reversed and released referral reward states with explicit fraud-review timing; only server-settled **whole** MB enters redeemable balance. Do not silently claw back available purchased money or create synthetic reward balances.
- [ ] **SPE-B09** Expose privacy-safe campaign metrics: total public verified views, target/delivered/remaining payable views, organic/referral split, valid/invalid attribution and earned/pending MB; no list of viewer identities to advertisers or sharers.
- [ ] **SPE-B10** Test duplicate visits, session clears, app/web cross-surface double counting, two links, self-view, bot bursts, legitimate shared IP, late invalidation, campaign exhaustion, offline, retries and app-link recovery.

## Delivery and scope guardrails
- Product choice: **no delay or frequency limit** on approved paid ads while demand is low. Future pacing/relevance work requires a separate owner decision; this programme must not sneak it in.
- The campaign's paid-view target and safety/financial admission are not forbidden “display limits”: they prevent budget overrun and unlawful/unreviewed publication.
- All shared UI changes should be one connected Status/mobile PR after server contracts stabilize. Avoid reopening Status cards separately for views, promotion badges, action row, images and spacing.
- Keep old UX-S05/S06 safety work intact, and retire only conflicting **free-human-composer prohibition**. Do not recreate human DM/calls or deleted creator features.
- Recheck latest main and active PRs before implementation; no destructive reset/force-push; source-only evidence is not provider qualification.
