# Citizenvoice early assessment publication

- Fewer than five active participating accounts: no numeric estimate, no count and no personal assessment in the public response.
- Five to nineteen: capture at most one fixed 25-percentage-point range per public subject and question. Release no earlier than the next UTC date. Never include an exact count, mean or raw distribution. A range is a historical observation, not live approval, and must display its observation date. No series of early snapshots, no early ranking and no retroactive reconstruction.
- At least twenty: retain the existing rounded aggregate format and history. Mature results take priority over the early range. If current results are suppressed later, the dated early observation may still be shown. Withdrawal changes current data; already recorded historical aggregates are not individual records.
- Public and authenticated endpoints use the same early snapshot. Account-specific writes retain tenant/account fencing. All parties, individuals and agencies receive identical publication rules; confidence remains separate from public-record assessment.
- This is privacy-coarsening and delayed publication, not a formal differential-privacy guarantee. Delayed fixed bands reduce direct differencing, but they cannot prevent every inference from outside knowledge or coordinated accounts. Do not label the results a representative public poll.
- No change to rewards, wallet, provider credentials, News or Knowledge. Real database migration replay, API tests, exact-head hosted CI and production readback are required.
