# Status public guest consent and nonpayable proof — 28 October 2026

**Status: implementation contract and draft disclosure; NOT legal approval or production activation.** The public guest collection, public landing and financial reward feature gates remain **OFF**. The draft below needs an appropriate Nigeria/privacy/counsel and product review, a real accessible web consent presentation, and anti-abuse admission before activation.

## Guest-facing notice (draft, plain language)

> You can read a public Masanawa Status post without installing the app, signing in, accepting a tracking cookie or earning anyone a reward. Simply visiting, sharing a link or loading a social preview is **not** a verified view. If you choose **Allow optional Status view verification**, Masanawa will set a secure, first-party, randomly generated cookie valid for up to 24 hours. It does not contain your name, phone number, account number, IP address or device fingerprint. We may keep a keyed, pseudonymous proof that you chose to view a specific public post, when you started and whether your browser reported that it was visible and engaged. A referral code, if you choose to keep it, may be associated with that proof. Browser claims alone do not prove a real human view: guest proofs remain **unverified, nonpayable** and cannot charge a sponsor or reward a sharer. You may decline and continue reading, choose to open a link without referral attribution, or **Withdraw optional Status view verification**. Withdrawal deletes these pending proofs and blocks reuse of the old cookie. Pending proof records are deleted no later than the normal 24-hour retention sweep; the revoked-token record expires after the cookie can no longer be used. Routine web security logs may be kept separately under the site's ordinary log policy. Contact Masanawa's published privacy channel for access/deletion concerns. This feature will not be activated until that channel, the notice, consent controls and the lawful basis have been reviewed.

## Processing boundaries

- The public `GET /status/:postId` crawler page is readable with JavaScript disabled. It sets no cookie, starts no proof and creates no financial or audience count. Its approved media route is separately verified. A rejected optional consent or a skipped referral code must leave that reading path working.
- Opt-in requires a same-origin `POST /v1/status/guest/consent` with an explicit JSON `{ "consent": true }` and an acceptable HTTPS `Origin`. There is no implicit consent from an unfurl, prefetch, link open, expired cookie or app installation. No wildcard CORS or cross-site cookie request is admitted. The cookie is `__Host-` scoped, `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Lax`, and limited to 24 hours, without a persistent ID.
- Per-consent keyed HMAC digests and post UUIDs are stored in `feed.guest_view_proofs`. Raw cookie/nonce, IP, fingerprint, GPS, phone, advertising ID, account identity and browser user-agent are **not** stored in this guest table. The referral field is only the nullable ID of an already-approved, live, one-level share link; it never reflects sharer or viewer details to the visitor.
- A server-timed six-second minimum plus a client report of foreground, 50% visibility and engagement is necessary to create `pending_review` evidence. It is **not sufficient** to establish a human or earn money. Every response explicitly reports `qualified: false` and `payable: false`. The evidence is entirely separate from `feed.qualified_views`, promotional budgets, immutable charges and reward ledgers; **no automatic promotion from one table into another exists**.
- Only one guest proof per consent/post is recorded (duplicate requests are idempotent); one consent cannot exceed 100 challenge starts in five minutes. No IP-wide per-user quota penalizes unrelated viewers on shared Nigerian carrier networks. Cookie clearing or bot-created new cookies can circumvent this signal; that limitation is why guest events are not promoted to payable views.
- `DELETE /v1/status/guest/consent` works even if new creation is disabled. It clears the browser cookie, deletes all matching guest proof rows and stores a keyed revocation until the captured cookie cannot still be valid. The sweeper deletes proofs after 24 hours and expired revocation rows in bounded batches. No unverified record is immutable financial/audit evidence.
- A narrowly scoped editorial reviewer API can list only pending proof IDs, post IDs, timestamps and whether a live referral was attached. It does **not** list consent/viewer HMACs, cookies, guest identities or network attributes. An independently authorized reviewer for the same tenant may mark evidence **held** or **rejected** with an enumerated reason; the one-time decision records server-derived reviewer attribution and is erased with the proof after its normal 24-hour retention. There is no **approve/verify**, reviewer bypass or payout endpoint. A validated independent identity/anti-bot pathway, accessibility review, appeal/false-positive process and fraud-risk qualification are preconditions to any public verified-count or commission interpretation.

## Operational and acceptance gates

1. This work supplies the consent cryptography, scoped PostgreSQL evidence/retention, anti-replay tests, fail-closed origin/JSON enforcement and separate nonpayable routes. A follow-on adds staff-only hold/reject review. Both visitor proof and admin review routes are registered under `enabled: () => false` even when Status promotions configuration is supplied.
2. A draft guest-facing screen at `/status/:postId/verify` now shows separate **Allow / Continue without optional verification / Withdraw** choices, keeps the public post readable, and performs no collection on GET. JavaScript requests a token only on Allow, starts the server challenge after consent and requires a continuous 50% visible foreground period plus an actual post interaction before submitting unverified observation. A restrictive per-response nonce CSP, no-store, noindex and no-referrer apply. The original public post remains JS-free, cookie-free and accessible if declined or scripting is unavailable; its link to this optional screen is itself **OFF**. This screen remains **a draft requiring product, counsel, accessible browser and low-bandwidth device review** before any activation.
3. A fraud/security reviewer must qualify guest identity equivalence, app/web deduplication, collusion/manual review, server-observable engagement, rate abuse, legitimate carrier NAT fairness, referer/CSRF and abuse recovery. Self-reported browser timing by itself does not qualify.
4. Privacy/legal review must approve the lawful basis, notice/cookie choice, operator contact, log policy, deletion procedures, and any geographic rollout. Do not represent this document as approval.
5. Provider-backed budgets/reward settlement, A07 cleared human imagery and A12 admin-funded Masana expense authority must be independently approved; public and financial flags remain off. No live user consent is collected by this work.
