# Direct NGN wallet cutover

The wallet holds NGN in integer kobo. Verified deposits credit it directly. Each successful logical AI reply costs ₦2, including voice plus its transcription as one reply; connected calls cost ₦1 per started second and setup/ringing are free. Send and Request transfer NGN. Buy Data is the former Redeem Data telecom journey, paying its reviewed NGN price directly. Telecom GB/MB is only an allowance size. Internal package purchase UI and new exchanges/tips/MB spends are retired.

Existing MB balances, immutable history and original pending operations retain their units. No automatic conversion or replacement cash welcome grant is authorized. New NGN operation tables, signatures, secure journals and namespaces isolate old records.

Wallet has Send, Request, Buy Data and Add Money funding instructions. Loading/offline/unavailable/frozen/account-switch states preserve truthful authority. Reviews show exact recipient, amount and available funds; disabled actions cannot submit. NGN holds are distinct from posted entries. Pending sends/orders use original receipt readback, never a fresh key. Data Start over resets only a draft; changed network/phone/bundle invalidates its review. Voice and text parts share one logical reply identity. Calls use trusted server-connected time and a funded deadline with original-admission recovery. Home has Like, Comment, Share and no Tip. Narrow, large-text and RTL layouts retain their controls.

Forward migrations extend current NGN accounting and add isolated calls/data/requests. Deposits keep signature verification, receiving-account binding, deduplication and reversal debt. Paired transfers debit and credit in one transaction with stable account lock order. Provider-uncertain data remains held until verified outcome. Reviewed data price is asserted against the server price before any hold.

Implementation validation is in progress. A complete combined TypeScript/build/test matrix plus exact-head hosted Flutter and PostgreSQL checks gates merge. Embedded PostgreSQL exercises actual SQL with serialized transactions, and must not be represented as multi-connection concurrency or live provider evidence. Local Flutter was blocked by automatic review after unexpected instance-metadata access; no retry or bypass is authorized. Production deployment, applied migrations and real provider settlements are not implied by source checks.
